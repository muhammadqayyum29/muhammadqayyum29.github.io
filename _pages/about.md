Welcome to my page! I am an Associate Professor in the School of Economics and Management at the Quanzhou University of Information Engineering. 

I got my Ph.D in economics from [Zhongnan University of Economics and Law](https://english.zuel.edu.cn/about/list.htm) in 2020. I was a posdoctoral researcher at the [Guangzhou University](https://english.gzhu.edu.cn/About_GU___Rankings/Rankings.htm) (2022-2024) and [Hainan University](https://en.hainanu.edu.cn/) (2020-2022). 

**Research interests**: Energy and environmental economics; machine learning in economics; digital economy; agricultural 
economics; international trade; economic complexity.
